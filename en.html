Research interests

Geometry and structure in infinite-dimensional spaces

01

Banach space theory and geometry

Linear structure, bases, and geometric properties of normed spaces.

02

Metric fixed point theory

Nonexpansive mappings and fixed point properties of Banach spaces.

03

Embedding theory

Embeddings of Banach spaces and connections between linear and geometric structures.

04

Nonlinear analysis

Existence and approximation problems in infinite-dimensional settings.

Manuscript and Lean formalization

A project on transfinite propagation systems and the ball fixed point property of C(K), accompanied by Lean code, build instructions, and a correspondence between the paper’s statements and their formal counterparts.
